Khanqah App Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

In short: we use your phone number to sign you in, your questions to the Shaykh are end-to-end encrypted, we don't sell your data or show ads, and you can ask us to delete your account at any time.

1. About this policy

This policy applies to the Khanqah Android app (package com.ennbi.khanqah), the official app of Khanqah Imdadia, published by EnnBi (“we”, “us”), Srinagar, India. It explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can have your data deleted.

2. Information we collect

You can browse and listen to public content without signing in. Signing in is needed for features tied to your account, such as Ask the Shaykh. When you use the app we collect:

  • Phone number — used to sign you in with a one-time password (OTP) sent by SMS. It is the identifier of your account.
  • Profile and preferences — your display name (if you set one), and your language (English or Urdu), theme, playback speed and skip-interval settings.
  • Listening activity — your playback position and completion status for each lecture, your playlists, and the content you have downloaded, so you can resume where you left off.
  • Ask the Shaykh messages — the questions you send (typed or recorded as a voice message) and the replies you receive. These are end-to-end encrypted; see section 4.
  • Security records — for Ask the Shaykh we log events such as sign-in, device registration and message sent/delivered/read, with a device identifier and IP address, to keep the service secure and detect abuse.
  • Bug reports — if the app sends a report, it can include your note, the screen you were on, app version, platform, recent app logs, network request details and error information, linked to your account.
  • Push notification token — a device token issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can notify you when a live broadcast starts, new content is uploaded, or the Shaykh replies to you.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier, and the app contains no ads.

3. Device permissions

  • Microphone — requested only when you tap to record a voice question or reply in Ask the Shaykh. Audio is recorded only while you are recording, and is encrypted on your device before it leaves it.
  • Notifications — to show live-broadcast, new-content and reply alerts. You can turn them off in the app's Profile settings or in Android settings.
  • Internet and foreground service — to stream audio and live video, and to keep playback running with a notification while the screen is off.

The app does not request access to your files, photos, media or storage.

4. Ask the Shaykh and encryption

Questions and replies are end-to-end encrypted. They are encrypted on your device with keys that stay on your device, and can be decrypted only by you and the Shaykh's devices. Our servers store the encrypted message (and encrypted audio file) together with technical details such as its size, sender, recipient and sent, delivered and read times, but cannot read the content.

Translation between Urdu and English for these messages, where used, runs on your device using Google ML Kit models; the message text is not sent to a translation service. Because the keys live on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing its data can make past messages unreadable.

5. How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To provide the app: sync your progress, playlists and settings, deliver Ask the Shaykh messages, and send notifications.
  • To fix bugs and keep the service reliable and safe.
  • To meet legal obligations.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising. We use these service providers to run the app, and they process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms:

  • Amazon Web Services (SNS) — sends the SMS one-time password to your phone number.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Cloudflare (R2 storage and CDN) — stores and serves lecture audio and the encrypted Ask the Shaykh audio files.
  • DigitalOcean — hosts our servers and database in Bangalore, India.

We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

7. How long we keep it

  • One-time passwords are stored only as a hash and expire after 10 minutes.
  • Your sign-in session stays active for up to one year unless you sign out.
  • Your account data, listening activity and Ask the Shaykh messages are kept for as long as your account exists, then deleted when you ask us to delete your account.
  • Bug reports are kept for as long as needed to diagnose and fix the issue.

8. Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it, including your profile, listening activity, playlists, and Ask the Shaykh messages. Email hr@ennbi.com from the address you wish to be contacted on, with the phone number of your account in the subject or body. We will verify the request and complete it within 30 days.

You can also sign out, clear the app's data or uninstall the app at any time to remove what is stored on your device. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access or correct your information; contact us at the same address.

9. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS, Ask the Shaykh content is end-to-end encrypted, and access tokens and one-time passwords are not stored in readable form. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us their phone number, email us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it last changed, and material changes will be reflected here.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email hr@ennbi.com or write to EnnBi, Srinagar, India.
